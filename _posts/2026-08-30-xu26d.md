abstract: " Power transforms are popular parametric methods for making data more Gaussian-like,
  and are widely used as preprocessing steps in statistical analysis and machine learning.
  However, we find that direct implementations of power transforms suffer from severe
  numerical instabilities, which can lead to incorrect results or even crashes. In
  this paper, we provide a comprehensive analysis of the sources of these instabilities
  and propose effective remedies. We further extend power transforms to the federated
  learning setting, addressing both numerical and distributional challenges that arise
  in this context. Experiments on real-world datasets demonstrate that our methods
  are both effective and robust, substantially improving stability compared to existing
  approaches. "
