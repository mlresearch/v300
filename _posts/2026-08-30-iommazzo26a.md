abstract: " We study the linear convergence of Frank-Wolfe algorithms over product
  polytopes. We analyze two condition numbers for the product polytope, namely the
  pyramidal width and the vertex-facet distance, based on the condition numbers of
  individual polytope components.  As a result, for convex objectives that are $\\mu$-Polyak-{Ł}ojasiewicz,
  we show linear convergence rates quantified in terms of the resulting condition
  numbers. We apply our results to the problem of approximately finding a feasible
  point in a polytope intersection in high-dimensions, and demonstrate the practical
  efficiency of our algorithms through empirical results. "
