abstract: " Techniques for \\emph{concept extraction}, such as sparse autoencoders
  and transcoders, aim to extract high-level symbolic concepts from low-level nonsymbolic
  representations. When these extracted concepts are used for downstream tasks such
  as model steering and unlearning, it is essential to understand their guarantees,
  or lack thereof. In this work, we present a unified theoretical framework for unsupervised
  concept extraction, in which we frame the task of concept extraction as identifying
  a generative model. We present a general meta-theorem for identifiability, which
  reduces the problem of establishing identifiability guarantees to the problem of
  characterizing the intersection of two sets. As we demonstrate on a range of widely-used
  approaches, this meta-theorem substantially simplifies the task of proving such
  guarantees, thus paving the way for the development of new, principled approaches
  for concept extraction. "
