abstract: " The central goal of active learning is to gather data that maximises downstream
  predictive performance, but popular approaches have limited flexibility in customising
  this data acquisition to different downstream problems and losses. We propose a
  rigorous loss-driven approach to Bayesian active learning that allows data acquisition
  to directly target the loss associated with a given decision problem. In particular,
  we show how any loss can be used to derive a unique objective for optimal data acquisition.
  Critically, we then show that any loss taking the form of a weighted Bregman divergence
  permits analytic computation of a central component of its corresponding objective,
  making the approach applicable in practice. In regression and classification experiments
  with a range of different losses, we find our approach reduces test losses relative
  to existing techniques. "
