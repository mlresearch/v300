abstract: " Enumerating Minimal Unsatisfiable Subsets (MUSes) is a fundamental task
  in constraint satisfaction problems (CSPs). Its major challenge is the exponential
  growth of the search space, which becomes particularly severe when satisfiability
  checks are expensive. Recent machine learning approaches reduce this cost for Boolean
  satisfiability problems but rely on explicit variable-constraint relationships,
  limiting their application domains. This paper proposes a domain-agnostic method
  to accelerate MUS enumeration using Hypergraph Neural Networks (HGNNs). The proposed
  method incrementally builds a hypergraph with constraints as vertices and MUSes
  enumerated until the current step as hyperedges, and employs an HGNN-based agent
  trained via reinforcement learning to minimize the number of satisfiability checks
  required to obtain an MUS. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of
  our approach in accelerating MUS enumeration, showing that our method can enumerate
  more MUSes within the same satisfiability check budget compared to conventional
  methods. "
