abstract: " Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) is a central component of modern
  LLM systems, particularly in scenarios where up-to-date information is crucial for
  accurately responding to user queries or when queries exceed the scope of the training
  data. The advent of time-series foundation models (TSFM), such as Chronos or Moirai,
  and the need for effective zero-shot forecasting performance across various time-series
  domains motivates the question: Do the benefits of RAG similarly carry over to time
  series forecasting? In this paper, we advocate that the dynamic and event-driven
  nature of time-series data makes RAG a crucial component of TSFMs and introduce
  a principled RAG framework for time-series forecasting, called Retrieval Augmented
  Forecasting (RAF). Within RAF, we develop efficient strategies for retrieving related
  time-series examples and incorporating them into the forecast. Through experiments
  and mechanistic studies, we demonstrate that RAF improves the forecasting accuracy
  across diverse time series domains and TSFMs, with gains that are more pronounced
  for larger models. "
