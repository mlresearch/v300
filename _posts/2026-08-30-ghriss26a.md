abstract: " Probabilistic relaxations of graph cuts offer a differentiable alternative
  to spectral clustering, enabling end-to-end and online learning without eigendecompositions,
  yet prior work centered on RatioCut and lacked general guarantees and principled
  gradients. We present a unified probabilistic framework that covers a wide class
  of cuts, including Normalized Cut. Our framework provides tight analytic upper bounds
  on expected discrete cuts via integral representations and Gauss hypergeometric
  functions with closed-form forward and backward. Together, these results deliver
  a rigorous, numerically stable foundation for scalable, differentiable graph partitioning
  covering a wide range of clustering and contrastive learning objectives. "
