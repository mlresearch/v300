abstract: " The Lasso has been widely used in a high-dimensional setting, but its
  estimation accuracy may become inadequate when the covariates are highly correlated
  or when the number of covariates is extremely large. To overcome this problem, we
  propose a novel preconditioner that adaptively induces a low-rank structure in the
  design matrix. The proposed preconditioner achieves a higher probability of sign
  correctness under some conditions. We establish theoretical guarantees showing that
  our method dominates the standard Lasso, and we further demonstrate its superiority
  over the correlation shifting. To validate its practical effectiveness, we conducted
  numerical experiments on synthetic and semi-real datasets, and the proposed method
  presented better performance than existing methods. "
