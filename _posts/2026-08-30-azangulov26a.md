abstract: " Classifier-Free Guidance (CFG) is a cornerstone of modern diffusion models,
  playing a pivotal role in conditional generation and enhancing the quality of unconditional
  samples. However, current approaches to CFG scheduling—determining the appropriate
  guidance weight—are largely heuristic and lack a solid theoretical foundation. This
  work addresses these limitations on two fronts. First, we provide a theoretical
  formalization that precisely characterizes the relationship between guidance strength
  and classifier confidence. Second, building on this insight, we introduce a stochastic
  optimal control framework that casts CFG scheduling as an adaptive optimization
  problem. In this formulation, guidance strength is not fixed but dynamically selected
  based on time, the current sample, and the conditioning class, either independently
  or in combination. By solving the resulting control problem, we establish a principled
  foundation for more effective guidance in diffusion models. "
