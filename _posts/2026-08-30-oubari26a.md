abstract: " Multi-component datasets with intricate dependencies challenge current
  generative modeling techniques. Existing Multi-component Variational AutoEncoders
  rely on simplified aggregation strategies that compromise structural coherence across
  generated components. We introduce the Gaussian Markov Random Field Multi-Component
  Variational AutoEncoder, embedding Gaussian Markov Random Fields into both prior
  and posterior distributions to explicitly model cross-component relationships. This
  enables richer representation and faithful reproduction of complex interactions.
  Empirically, our model achieves state-of-the-art performance on a synthetic Copula
  dataset designed for intricate component relationships, competitive results on PolyMNIST,
  and significantly enhanced structural coherence on the real-world BIKED dataset,
  demonstrating its suitability for applications demanding robust multi-component
  coherence. "
