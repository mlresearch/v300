title: " PAC-Bayesian Bounds on Constrained $f$-Entropic Risk Measures "

abstract: " PAC generalization bounds on the risk, when expressed in terms of the
  expected loss, are often insufficient to capture imbalances between subgroups in
  the data. To tackle this limitation, we introduce a new family of risk measures,
  called constrained $f$-entropic risk measures, which enable finer control over distributional
  shifts and subgroup imbalances via $f$-divergences, and include the Conditional
  Value at Risk (CVaR), a well-known risk measure. We derive both classical and disintegrated
  PAC-Bayesian generalization bounds for this family of risks, providing the first
  disintegrated PAC-Bayesian guarantees beyond standard risks. Building on this theory,
  we design a self-bounding algorithm minimizing our bounds directly, yielding models
  with guarantees at the subgroup level. We empirically demonstrate the usefulness
  of our approach. "
