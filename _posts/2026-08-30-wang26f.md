abstract: " We introduce a novel particle-based algorithm for end-to-end training
  of latent diffusion models. We reformulate the training task as minimizing a free
  energy functional and obtain a gradient flow that does so. By approximating the
  latter with a system of interacting particles, we obtain the algorithm, which we
  underpin theoretically by providing error guarantees. The novel algorithm compares
  favorably in experiments with previous particle-based methods and variational inference
  analogues. "
