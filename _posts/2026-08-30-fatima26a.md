abstract: " Complex data are often represented as a graph, which in turn can often
  be viewed as a realisation of a random graph, such as an inhomogeneous random graph
  model (IRG). For general fast goodness-of-fit tests in high dimensions, kernelised
  Stein discrepancy (KSD) tests are a powerful tool. Here, we develop a KSD-type test
  for IRG models that can be carried out with a single observation of the network.
  The test applies to networks of any size, but is particularly relevant for small
  networks for which asymptotic tests are not warranted. We also provide theoretical
  guarantees. "
